Princeton, Massachusetts

Hotels & Boarding Houses of the Resort Era, 1887–1890

The map opens on a reconstruction of the town as it stood in 1887–1890 — period roads, the 1871 railroad, the town boundary, real elevation contours of Mount Wachusett, the three villages (Centre, West Village, and the East Princeton chair-mill village), their civic and industrial landmarks and their buildings. Use the layers control (top-right) to switch to the modern map, or the slider to fade the two. Click any pin, building, or contour for details.
